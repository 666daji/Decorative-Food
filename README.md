> **Make your food placeable.** Decorate your world with edible blocks — stack your snacks, line up your stews and bottles, and turn a kitchen corner into a proper food display.

## Overview

**Decorative Food** is a lightweight, vanilla-flavored mod that lets food items be placed in the world as **3D blocks**. Drop an apple on the table, pile up some bread, or leave a bowl of stew at the counter — everything keeps the original Minecraft pixel-art look and fits right into your builds.

It adds **no new game rules** and doesn't disturb existing gameplay. The original food items are still real items; they're simply made placeable and stackable on top of one another.

## Features

- **Place any food as a block** — hold the item and right-click a surface, just like placing a normal block.
- **Stackable food** — sneak-right-click a placed pile with the same food to add one more; each food has its own maximum stack size (apples up to 5, cookies up to 7, etc.).
- **Sneak-to-interact** — you can still add to / take from a food block while sneaking, so stacking never fights with placing.
- **Vanilla art style** — models and textures deliberately match the original Minecraft look.

## Supported food

The following vanilla items can be placed and stacked (over 45 food types in total):

- **Snacks:** Cookie, Apple, Melon Slice, Bread, Dried Kelp
- **Vegetables:** Beetroot, Potato, Baked Potato, Poisonous Potato, Carrot, Sweet Berries, Glow Berries
- **Golden foods:** Golden Apple, Enchanted Golden Apple, Golden Carrot, Glistering Melon Slice
- **Meats:** Chicken, Cooked Chicken, Beef, Cooked Beef, Mutton, Cooked Mutton, Porkchop, Cooked Porkchop, Rabbit, Cooked Rabbit
- **Fish:** Cod, Cooked Cod, Salmon, Cooked Salmon, Pufferfish, Tropical Fish
- **Stews & bowls:** Rabbit Stew, Mushroom Stew, Beetroot Soup, Suspicious Stew, Bowl
- **Buckets:** Bucket, Water Bucket, Milk Bucket, Lava Bucket
- **Other:** Pumpkin Pie, Chorus Fruit, Egg, Spider Eye, Totem of Undying, Potion, Glass Bottle, Honey Bottle

## How to use

1. **Place** — hold a food item and right-click on a surface (e.g. the ground or a table).
2. **Stack** — *sneak*-right-click the placed food with the **same** item to add one more, up to that food's max.
3. **Take** — *sneak*-right-click the pile with any other item or an empty hand to pull one food back out.
